# Terminology Note — "Spatial"

### What does "spatial" mean in video/image terminology?

In simple English:

> **Spatial = related to where something is located in the image.**

For example, when talking about pixels:

```text id="2v7r3k"
Pixel
  ↓
information at a particular location
  ↓
where something is in the image
```

So:

```text id="x9k2pa"
Spatial information
        =
information about
WHERE things are in the image
```

### Example

Consider a face:

```text id="m5z8qc"
   👁     👁
      👃
   👄
```

The fact that the eyes are **above** the mouth is spatial information.

Likewise:

* Left vs right → spatial
* Above vs below → spatial
* Object position → spatial
* Shape/edges → spatial
* Fine image details → spatial

### In our video discussions

When someone says:

> **"More spatial samples"**

think:

> **"More pixel locations."**

When someone says:

> **"Spatial detail"**

think:

> **"Detail about what things look like and where they are in the image."**

### Remember

```text id="f2m7qa"
SPATIAL
   ↓
WHERE?

TEMPORAL
   ↓
WHEN?
```

We'll encounter **temporal** soon when we study how things change between frames.

**Simple translation for your notes:**

> **Spatial = related to position/location within an image.**
